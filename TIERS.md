# Provtara tiers — Free, Basic, Pro, Premium

**Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Rule:** money buys volume and polish. Money never buys a fake skill.

Step 6 (LLM rewrite) stays **off** until an API key is set. Every tier still gets Pack A from the deterministic writer. The firewall and the “you click Submit” rule apply to all four.

Prices below are the recommended list (22 August 2026). Nigeria pays NGN monthly. Everyone else pays USD. Do not live-convert NGN from USD — they are two markets.

---

## What every tier gets (never paywall these)

- Browse IT vacancies (Nigeria, Web3, remote, the rest)
- Search and on-site / remote / anywhere
- Upload a résumé and confirm the profile
- Gap table (met / not met) before any pack
- Truth firewall
- Worked-example templates on the right
- Official apply URL — you submit

If a paid plan skipped the gap table or softened the firewall, the brand is dead.

---

## What money is actually buying

1. **How many packs per month** — each pack is a job you are qualified for, with a résumé + letter.
2. **How many live confirmed profiles** — one CV vs several (backend vs mobile vs Web3).
3. **How far Auto-apply can queue** — still no Submit; just how many official tabs we prepare in one sitting.
4. **File format** — markdown vs Word/PDF.
5. **Alerts** — new Nigerian / Web3 jobs that pass *your* gate.
6. **Step 6 polish** — only when an API key exists, and only on Pro and Premium (API cost).

---

## Free

**Who:** try it, criticise it, one real application.

- 1 confirmed profile
- 2 packs per month
- Auto-apply prepares **1** job at a time
- Download: markdown only
- Weekly digest of up to 3 qualified jobs (after résumé confirm)
- No Step 6 even after a key exists
- Templates: view on site; limited downloads (e.g. 3 titles / week)

Free must still be *usable*. Two honest packs beat a hundred fake ones.

---

## Basic

**Who:** actively applying, one career track.

- 1 confirmed profile
- 10 packs per month
- Auto-apply prepares up to **5** jobs per sitting
- Download: markdown + DOCX
- Email/WhatsApp alert: Nigerian IT jobs that pass the gate (daily digest, not spam)
- No Step 6

This is the “I will actually use this to hunt” plan.

---

## Pro

**Who:** serious search across markets (Nigeria + remote + Web3).

- 2 confirmed profiles (example: backend CV and Web3 CV — still each confirmed, still no invented overlap)
- 30 packs per month
- Auto-apply prepares up to **10** jobs per sitting (today’s workshop cap)
- Download: markdown + DOCX + PDF
- Alerts: Nigeria + Web3 + one extra market you pick (US, UK, Kenya, …)
- **Step 6 on**, when `XAI_API_KEY` (or a fallback key) is set. Firewall still discards a lying rewrite.
- Pack history: last 90 days

Pro is where the optional model earns its keep.

---

## Premium

**Who:** consultants, career-switchers running two tracks, or people applying every week.

- 5 confirmed profiles
- 80 packs per month
- Auto-apply sitting: 10 jobs (same cap as Pro — do not sell “we submit 80 for you”)
- Same downloads as Pro
- Alerts: all markets you follow, faster (a few hours, not a daily dump)
- Step 6 on, same firewall
- Optional Web3 proof later: paste a tx hash or GitHub repo; we fetch it; only then may that evidence stay on the pack
- Priority when boards are slow to refresh
- Human review slot later (you or a reviewer looks at one pack / month) — not required for v1

Premium is convenience and volume. It is not “we will make you look senior.”

---

## What we refuse to sell at any price

- Inventing Kubernetes / Solidity / years / a degree
- Clicking Submit
- Storing passwords for employer sites
- Turning off the gap table
- A “bypass the gate” toggle
- Selling confirmed résumés to recruiters (default off, forever unless the candidate releases a pack)

---

## How this maps to cost (so the ladder is honest)

| Cost we actually pay | Covered by |
|---|---|
| Hosting, job ingest | Free can exist; Basic starts to pay the lights |
| DOCX/PDF rendering | Basic+ |
| Alerts | Basic+ (volume), Pro/Premium (more markets) |
| LLM tokens (Step 6) | Pro and Premium only, and only with a key |
| Extra stored profiles | Pro / Premium |

If Step 6 has no key, Pro and Premium still make sense: more packs, PDF, alerts. They just skip the polish line on the pack screen (“Rewrite unavailable — showing the evidenced pack”).

## Recommended list prices (lock these)

FX around this date: official ~₦1,347 / $1, street ~₦1,400. **Do not multiply.** A $19 tool at official FX is ~₦25,600, which is too high for most Nigerian job hunts. NGN is a local price, like Netflix Nigeria vs Netflix US.

| Plan | USD / month | USD / year (2 months free) | NGN / month | NGN / year (2 months free) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | ₦0 | ₦0 |
| Basic | **$9** | **$90** | **₦5,000** | **₦50,000** |
| Pro | **$19** | **$190** | **₦12,000** | **₦120,000** |
| Premium | **$39** | **$390** | **₦25,000** | **₦250,000** |

Charge the card’s home market: Nigerian billing details → NGN (Paystack). US/UK/EU/etc. → USD (Stripe). Kenya/Ghana/South Africa start on USD until you add local rails; do not dump them on the Nigerian price.

**Why these numbers**

- Neighbours in 2026: Teal ~$29/mo, Rezi ~$29, Huntr ~$40, Jobscan ~$50, LazyApply-class mass-apply $99+. We do **not** click Submit, so we sit **under Teal**, not next to LazyApply.
- Basic at $9 / ₦5,000 is a one-month hunt: 10 packs, Word file, Nigeria alerts. Cheap enough to try without a committee.
- Pro at $19 / ₦12,000 is the default paid plan (two profiles, PDF, Step 6 when a key exists). Still cheaper than Teal+.
- Premium at $39 / ₦25,000 is volume + later proof-of-work. Still under Jobscan. It is not “unlimited apply.”
- Annual = 10× monthly. People in an 8-week search should take monthly and cancel. People who hire and hunt every year take annual.

**Per pack (monthly)**

- Basic: $0.90 / ₦500
- Pro: $0.63 / ₦400
- Premium: $0.49 / ₦312

**Do not**

- Do not raise Free’s pack cap to “unlimited” — that is how Pro never sells.
- Do not put Step 6 on Basic even after a key exists (token cost).
- Do not auto-hike NGN when FX moves 2%. Revisit NGN if the naira moves ~20% and stays there.
